AI Longform Editor

AI Longform Editor is a private desktop tool used by a single channel owner to edit livestream recordings into long-form videos and upload them to the owner's own YouTube channels.

It signs in with Google only to upload videos, thumbnails, captions and video details (title, description, publishing time) to channels that the owner manages. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

AI Longform Editor 是頻道主自用的桌面工具：把直播錄播剪成長影片，並上傳到頻道主自己管理的 YouTube 頻道。 它只用 Google 登入來上傳影片、縮圖、字幕和影片資訊，不對外提供服務。
